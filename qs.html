How to Play

  • Answer each question and click Save . You can edit your answers until the next day.
  • How many coins will I earn today?
    Earn 5 🪙 if your forecast is within 30 coins of your actual total for that day.
  • Which will be the toughest category?
    Earn 5 🪙 for forecasting the daily category where players earn the fewest total coins.
  • If the daily categories tie for coins earned, no answers are correct for that question.
  • Your maximum Questions score is 10 🪙 . Check your score once the day's results are complete.
ⓘ Dates shown in Pacific Time (PT)
